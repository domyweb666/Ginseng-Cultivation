# 回覆 Apple 審查：1.4.1 健康資訊沒有出處

> 2026-11-23 被退：1.0（34），iPad Air 11 吋（M3），Guideline 1.4.1，點名「輸入體組成」。
> 這份是草稿，多米點頭才送。`BUILD` 換成新的建置號再貼。
> 貼的地方：App Store Connect →「回覆 App 審查」。附一張輸入體組成表單的截圖和一張出處頁的截圖。

## 英文（貼這段）

```
Hello,

Thank you for the review. We have added citations for all health-related calculations in the new build, 1.0 (BUILD).

WHERE TO FIND THEM
1. On the 境界 (Realm) tab, tap 手動輸入體組成 (or the manual entry button on the source row) to open 輸入體組成 (Enter body composition), the screen named in your message.
2. Directly below the input fields, with no scrolling needed, tap 計算依據與出處 (Calculation basis and sources).
The same page also opens from:
- 分析 (Analysis) tab > 境界 segment > the card at the bottom
- 分析 (Analysis) tab > 功法 segment > the card at the bottom
- 設定 (Settings) tab > 關於 (About) > 計算依據與出處

WHAT THE PAGE CONTAINS
It begins with a notice that the app is for logging and entertainment, is not for diagnosis or treatment, does not replace a doctor's advice, and that users should ask a doctor before making health decisions. Each calculation is then explained, with tappable links to its sources:
- FFMI and height correction: Kouri et al. 1995 (PubMed 7496846)
- Realm thresholds: Kouri et al. 1995; Kyle et al. 2003 (PubMed 12831945); Harty et al. 2019 (PubMed 31238804)
- Body fat percentage ranges: Kyle et al. 2003; American Council on Exercise
- Body fat scale accuracy and measuring conditions: Tinsley et al. 2022 (PMC9124033); Kasper et al. 2021 (PMC8065383); ESPEN guideline part II, Kyle et al. 2004 (PubMed 15556267)
- Estimated one-repetition maximum: Macarilla et al. 2022 (PMC9465738); Reynolds et al. 2006 (PubMed 16937972)
- Strength level thresholds: Strength Level standards

The page also states which thresholds are game settings chosen by us rather than medical classifications.

OTHER CHANGES
- The height correction coefficient now matches the cited paper (6.3).
- We removed the "low body fat" label, because we could not cite a source for it.
- We reworded the FFMI explanation and removed an uncited claim from the entry form.

Screenshots of the entry form and the sources page are attached. Please let us know if anything else is needed.
```
