# 審查回覆 2026-11-27（1.0（40），Guideline 2.3.7）

退件原因：商店截圖寫了價格。Apple 說「免費」「優惠」也算價格。

改了什麼：第 8 張截圖（十一種造型）的副標原本寫「原版免費。記錄、算境界全部免費，付費的只有造型」，上面還有一顆「付費造型」標。副標改成「水墨、鎏金、青花瓷，境界碑和慶祝畫面跟著換」，標拿掉。另外七張本來就沒有價格字樣。app 本身沒改，還是 1.0（40）。

描述裡的「免費」不用拿掉：退件信寫想講價格可以放在描述裡。

## 貼在送審單回覆的英文

Hello,

Thank you for the review. We removed all pricing references from the app screenshots.

The 8th screenshot previously said that the original portrait style and all tracking features are free and that only the portrait styles are paid, and it had a "paid styles" label. Its caption now only names the portrait styles, and the label has been removed. None of the other screenshots mention price.

The app binary is unchanged (version 1.0, build 40).

Thank you.
